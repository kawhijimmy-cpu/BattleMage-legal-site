1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android game Battle Mage (package name com.SPIELPHANTOMLIMITED.BattleMage), operated by SPIELPHANTOMLIMITED ("we", "us", or "the developer"). The store entity, support email, and public contact details displayed on the Google Play listing may be updated from time to time; the contact email currently shown on the store listing is kawhijimmy@spielphantom.com.

3. Information we collect and how it is collected

Depending on how you use the game, the following categories of information may be collected or processed:

  • Device and technical information. Device model, operating system version, language, region, IP address, network type, app version, unique device or app identifiers, the Google Advertising ID (AAID), and similar identifiers. This information is collected automatically by the app and by the Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google AdMob.
  • Advertising and interaction data. Ads shown, ad impressions, clicks, rewarded video completion, ad format and ad unit, your interactions with ads, consent status, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad performance data. This information is collected by Google AdMob and its ad technology providers.
  • Game progress and settings. Local save data, in-game progress, unlocked characters or items, settings, and gameplay statistics stored on your device. If you use Google Play Games cloud save, this data may be uploaded to Google's Saved Games service and associated with your Google Play Games account.
  • Google Play Games account information (optional). Player ID, display name or nickname, avatar (if provided), and cloud save data, only if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games.
  • Purchase information (if in-app purchases are added in the future). Google Play order or purchase token information. The current version does not provide in-app purchases. If this changes, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data Safety form.
  • Support communications. If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, message content, screenshots, and device details.
  • Diagnostics and security data. Crash logs, performance data, fraud-prevention signals, and security-related information collected by the app, Google Play, or AdMob to keep the game and advertising secure. The current version does not use a separate crash-reporting SDK, but Google and AdMob may collect ad-related diagnostics.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories such as health data, precise geolocation, contacts, camera images, microphone audio, SMS messages, or photos. The game does not request those permissions.

5. Advertising, Google AdMob, and consent

We use Google AdMob to display rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. AdMob and its ad technology providers may collect and process information as described in Google's privacy policy and in Google's page "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

Personalized and non-personalized advertising

  • Personalized advertising uses information such as your Advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and previous interactions to show ads that may be more relevant to you.
  • Non-personalized advertising does not use your previous ad interactions to personalize the ads, but may still use contextual information, device information, and the Advertising ID for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, and fraud prevention, as permitted by law.

Consent in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP), to ask for your consent before personalized advertising. The consent message lets you choose "Consent", "Do not consent", or "Manage options". If you consent, you may see personalized ads. If you decline, we will not serve personalized ads to you; you may see non-personalized ads or no ads, depending on your choice and applicable law.

You can still play the game if you decline personalized advertising. However, rewarded ads may not be available, so ad-based rewards may be unavailable. We do not require you to consent to personalized advertising in order to play.

How to change or withdraw consent

You can change or withdraw your advertising consent at any time:

  • Open the game, go to Settings, and choose Ad Privacy Settings (also called Privacy Options). This opens the Google UMP consent form again.
  • If the app uses Google's ad unit deployment, the developer will also provide the Google revocation link in the game or in this policy. You can use that link to change your consent choices.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings (for example, Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can also visit Google Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com/.
Note: Google and its ad technology providers process some advertising data under their own policies. We do not control their independent data processing or retention periods. Please review Google's privacy policy for details.
